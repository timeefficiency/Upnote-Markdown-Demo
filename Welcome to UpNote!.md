## Welcome to UpNote!

Thank you for choosing UpNote — we hope you enjoy the app.

<br>

UpNote helps you take notes quickly and stay organized. Notes are saved automatically, and you can view past edits with [version history](https://help.getupnote.com/more/restore-a-note-version).

<br>

Here are some tips to get the most out of UpNote.

<br>

### Get Started with UpNote:

- [x] [Download UpNote](https://getupnote.com/#download) on your phone, tablet, and computer.
- [ ] Create a free account and sign in on all your devices.
- [ ] Start writing your first note.
- [ ] Explore our [User Guide](https://help.getupnote.com) to learn about UpNote’s features.

<br>

### Write your thoughts with rich formatting:

- Use list and nested list, like this:
    - Write text in **bold,** _italic,_ underline, ~~strike-through~~.
    - Highlight text in multiple colors: ==orange==, ==red==, ==blue==, ==yellow==, ==green== and ==purple==.
- Select your preferred font in Settings.

### Link between notes 🔗

- You can link to other notes, for example see [[Explore UpNote features]].
- To link to another note, simply type `[[` and you can select the note you want to refer to. (You can find out more about note linking [here](https://medium.com/upnote/bi-directional-links-in-upnote-63631bd36b2a)).
